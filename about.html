Stray Kids is a self-producing boy group formed by JYP Entertainment in 2017. They debuted on March 25, 2018 with District 9 — a song about breaking expectations and choosing your own identity. The group consists of eight members—Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N—and debuted in 2018, quickly gaining international fame for their distinctive sound and experimental style.
Their music speaks about youth, confusion, growth, anxiety, and finding yourself in a noisy world. Not perfect heroes, not unreachable idols — just eight people running forward together.

The name Stray Kids means wandering youth searching for their place in life. And the fandom name STAY exists because they promised never to leave each other behind.
Through every era, every sound, every stage — their story is about becoming who you are meant to be. Not following a path. But creating one.
